# Project Vision

## Vision Statement
AstraVoyage will become a trusted, scalable, and secure OTA platform that delivers seamless flight booking experiences for individual travelers, corporate clients, agents, and administrators.

## Business Objective
The platform will reduce friction in travel booking workflows by combining modern digital experiences with reliable backend services, strong compliance controls, and flexible integrations for third-party suppliers.

## Strategic Goals
- Deliver a reliable flight booking MVP within the first release cycle.
- Establish a modular architecture that supports future product expansion.
- Provide differentiated experiences for customers, agents, and corporate users.
- Ensure strong security, auditability, and operational resilience.

## Success Criteria
- Booking completion rate above target for the MVP launch.
- Average booking confirmation latency remains low and predictable.
- Support workflows are operable with clear audit trails.
- The platform can onboard additional travel verticals without major rework.

## Stakeholders
- Business leadership
- Product management
- Engineering and architecture
- Operations and support
- Finance and compliance
- Travel suppliers and payment partners
