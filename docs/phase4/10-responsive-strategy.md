# Responsive Strategy

## Breakpoints
- Mobile: < 768px
- Tablet: 768px - 1023px
- Desktop: >= 1024px
- Wide Desktop: >= 1440px

## Strategy
- Prioritize primary booking tasks on mobile first.
- Reflow content fluidly rather than hiding essential actions.
- Use adaptive card layouts for search results and dashboards.
- Ensure touch targets remain sufficiently large on small screens.

## Layout Behavior
- Navigation collapses on smaller screens.
- Secondary information moves into accordions or expandable sections.
- Tables convert to stacked cards where appropriate.
