# Customer Screens

## Core Customer Journeys
- Home / Discover Flights
- Flight Search Results
- Flight Details
- Passenger Information
- Payment and Review
- Booking Confirmation
- Booking History
- Profile and Preferences
- Support Center

## Screen Goals
- Reduce friction from search to purchase
- Build confidence with transparent pricing and policies
- Encourage rebooking and repeat visits
- Make support and cancellation actions obvious and calm
