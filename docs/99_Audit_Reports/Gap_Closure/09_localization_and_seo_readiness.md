# Localization and SEO Readiness

## Objective
Support global reach and discoverability for the OTA platform.

## Localization Requirements
- Support multi-language content and locale-aware formatting.
- Store content strings in a localization-friendly structure.
- Enable date, time, currency, and number formatting based on locale.
- Ensure UI text and validation errors are translatable.

## SEO Requirements
- Provide metadata for home, search, and booking-related pages.
- Ensure public content surfaces follow accessible and crawlable information architecture.
- Support canonical URLs and structured data where applicable.
- Maintain a content governance process for marketing and public pages.
