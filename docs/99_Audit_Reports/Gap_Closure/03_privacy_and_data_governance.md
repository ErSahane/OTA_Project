# Privacy and Data Governance

## Objective
Establish the minimum privacy and data governance controls needed for enterprise travel products.

## Privacy Controls
- Classify data by sensitivity: public, internal, confidential, restricted.
- Apply data minimization so only required data is collected.
- Support consent capture and consent revocation for marketing and profiling use cases.
- Define retention periods for user, booking, payment, and audit records.
- Define deletion workflows for account closure and data subject requests.

## Data Governance Requirements
- Maintain record of data owners and processors.
- Restrict access to personal and payment-related data by role.
- Redact personal data in logs and monitoring outputs.
- Ensure audit logging is available to demonstrate access and change history.

## Recommended Policies
- Privacy notice and consent policy
- Data retention and deletion policy
- Data access review policy
- Incident notification policy for data breaches
