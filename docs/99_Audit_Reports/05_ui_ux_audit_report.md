# UI/UX Audit Report

## Scope
Audit of the UI/UX design system, screen planning, navigation, accessibility, and user flows.

## Overall Assessment
The UI/UX package is modern, structured, and thoughtfully aligned to a customer-first travel experience. It covers the main customer and admin journeys and offers a solid basis for consistent design.

## Strengths
- Clear component inventory and design principles
- Strong focus on customer journey clarity and trust
- Accessibility goals are explicitly defined
- Mobile and responsive strategy are addressed

## Issues Found

### 1. Content Strategy and Localization are Not Fully Defined
- Severity: Medium
- Description: The UI design documentation lacks a detailed content strategy for localization, tone, and regional adaptation.
- Business Impact: Could affect adoption in international markets.
- Technical Impact: Inconsistent implementation of copy and translation workflows.
- Recommendation: Specify localization and content governance patterns.
- Suggested Fix: Add a localization strategy with content keys, translation workflow, and region-specific content guidance.
- Priority: Medium

### 2. SEO and Search Intent Strategy Are Not Covered
- Severity: Medium
- Description: The design documentation does not address SEO considerations for public marketing and content surfaces.
- Business Impact: Reduced discoverability in search engines.
- Technical Impact: Missing metadata and content architecture planning.
- Recommendation: Include SEO requirements for public surfaces and landing pages.
- Suggested Fix: Add a public content and SEO guideline section covering metadata, crawlability, and page structure.
- Priority: Medium

### 3. Design Handoff Detail Could Be stronger for Implementation
- Severity: Low
- Description: The Figma-ready documentation is useful but not yet detailed enough for full implementation handoff.
- Business Impact: Slight risk of design drift and missed interaction details.
- Technical Impact: More interpretation required by developers and designers.
- Recommendation: Expand handoff notes with component states, spacing references, and interaction microcopy.
- Suggested Fix: Add component specification templates and interaction notes for each major screen.
- Priority: Low
