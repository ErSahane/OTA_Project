# Monitoring Strategy

## Objectives
Provide visibility into platform health, business operations, and security events.

## Monitoring Areas
- Service availability and latency
- Error rates and exception trends
- Payment and booking success rates
- Supplier integration health
- Infrastructure utilization
- Security alert conditions

## Recommended Approach
- Metrics, logs, and traces unified in a central observability platform
- Alerting tied to SLOs and business KPIs
- Dashboards for engineering, operations, and leadership
- Synthetic monitoring for critical customer journeys

## Operational Response
- Incident management playbooks
- Escalation paths for critical failures
- Post-incident reviews for continuous improvement
