# Glossary

## Terms
- Booking: a reservation created for one or more travelers.
- Booking Item: a component or segment within a booking.
- Customer: a user who books travel services.
- Traveler: a person associated with a booking.
- Payment: the financial transaction representing booking settlement.
- Refund: a financial reversal or credit associated with a booking or payment.
- Supplier: an external partner providing inventory or service.
- Fare Rule: the pricing and eligibility rule associated with an itinerary option.
- Audit Event: a record of a significant system or business action.
- Soft Delete: a logical deletion that preserves record history.
