# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Purpose
AstraVoyage will provide a digital marketplace for flight bookings with strong support for customer self-service and business operations.

## 2. Business Goals
- Increase conversion from search to booking.
- Reduce support overhead through self-service tools.
- Introduce a clear path for agent and corporate adoption.
- Enable future expansion to additional travel products.

## 3. Scope
### In Scope for MVP
- Search for available flights
- Compare itineraries
- Select fares and ancillaries
- Secure booking flow
- Payment initiation and confirmation
- Booking cancellation and refund handling
- Admin monitoring and support actions

### Out of Scope for MVP
- Hotel, bus, visa, and insurance modules
- Advanced loyalty programs
- Full dynamic pricing engine
- Offline booking channels

## 4. Business Processes
- Customer searches flights and reviews options.
- Customer selects itinerary and enters traveler details.
- Customer completes payment.
- Booking is confirmed and delivered to the customer.
- Support teams can view and manage changes or refunds.

## 5. Key Business Rules
- Fare rules must be clearly displayed before purchase.
- Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on supplier policy.
- Sensitive customer data must be protected and logged according to policy.
- All financial transactions must be traceable.

## 6. Assumptions and Dependencies
- Supplier APIs will provide itinerary and fare data.
- Payment gateway integration will be available for the MVP.
- Operational support processes will be defined before launch.
